What is the Animal Face Test?

This test uses Google's Teachable Machine, an open-source AI, to analyze your facial features. Our AI has been trained on tens of thousands of images to identify common "animal face types" in humans.

It's a fun way to discover your hidden charms and share the results with your friends. Facial analysis (physiognomy) has long been used to understand personality traits, and this AI brings a modern twist to that tradition.
